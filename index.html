Measurement boundaries and implementation scope

The PC runs the existing reference encoder. Physical Cortex-A9 firmware maps and demaps encoded bits, returning every I/Q symbol and recovered bit over UART. The exact returned demapped bits are sent back to the selected physical decoder. This is digital loopback with a modem round trip followed by decoder and trace transactions, not RF transport, OFDM, channel estimation or an ADC/DAC link. PL Compute means all five decoder algorithms run in FPGA logic; ARM still handles modem, UART, control and DMA. Hybrid follows the paper's decoder partition on XC7Z010, rather than its UltraScale+ MPSoC.

Decoder timing uses three repetitions after one warm-up, with data resident on the board. UART, modem and trace replay are excluded and reported separately. Stage times overlap. CPU cycles mean elapsed CPU clock cycles, not retired instructions. CPU load, physical watts and energy efficiency are not fabricated. Existing SPEC-CHECK assumptions remain; shared reference agreement does not prove LDACS standards conformance or a radio real-time deadline.
