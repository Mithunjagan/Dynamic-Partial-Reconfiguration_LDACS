How to read these benchmarks

Units: 1,000 µs = 1 ms; 1,000 ms = 1 second. B means bytes; Mbit/s means one million bits per second. Coding rate 1/2 means one information bit for every two coded bits. PS: all five decoder stages on Cortex-A9. PL compute: all five in fabric; ARM still handles modem, UART and control. Hybrid: Helical and PRBS on ARM; Viterbi, Block and Reed–Solomon in fabric. All entries for a coding profile use the same freshly prepared padded frame.

Every measurement is collected after this campaign starts. Measurements from before the campaign and stored implementation/power reports are excluded. A resumed campaign preserves its original measurement times and collects only missing entries. Counters are verified against raw response values; returned stage buffers are hash-checked. Shared-model agreement is not independent LDACS standards conformance.

Three repetitions follow one warm-up. Firmware supplies aggregate/min/max counters, not individual latency samples; p95/p99 are unavailable. Clock rates use firmware-reported divisors and a nominal reference. Elapsed CPU clock cycles do not indicate CPU load or retired instructions.

The page polls once per second. A hardware result appears only when its actual UART/trace transaction completes.

Measured power, energy, CPU utilization, RF BER-versus-SNR, continuous streaming, and fresh LUT/FF/BRAM/DSP allocation require additional instrumentation or a fresh implementation run. No values are fabricated for these gaps.

Raw evidence will be saved in a new timestamped campaign directory.
